About our Studio

Ladies Only Sanctuary

Our Vision & Design

Lotas Makeup Studio & Salon is a premium, specialized space created specifically and exclusively for females. We believe that every woman deserves a relaxing, secure, and comfortable atmosphere where she can untangle from daily stress and receive top-tier self-care treatments.

Unlike general family salons, our strictly females-only policy ensures full privacy and peace of mind for all our ladies and girls. Our sanctuary is fully air-conditioned (A/C facility), providing a serene escape from Bhopal's heat, accompanied by soothing music and a luxurious modern ambiance.

We focus on delivering high-end results using premium quality cosmetics combined with highly affordable pricing. Luxury is no longer an expensive choice at Lotas Studio.

Meet the Founder & Lead Artist

Tanu

Certified Hair, Skin, & Makeup Professional

With a deep passion for aesthetics and styling, Tanu has dedicated over a decade to the beauty industry. She is a fully certified professional who has completed advanced diplomas in makeup, hair designing, skincare, and therapeutic facials at the prestigious Nikky Bawa Parlour—one of the largest and most renowned salon academies in Central India.

Before establishing Lotas Makeup Studio, Tanu refined her professional techniques by working alongside industry veterans and collaborating with top-tier brands, including Matrix Professional Salons and Nikki Bawa studios. This rich experience allows her to understand client requirements deeply, custom-tailoring haircuts and makeup profiles to enhance each client's unique facial structure.

Under her guidance, the entire salon staff is trained to maintain rigorous standards of hygiene, gentle care, and professional handling.
