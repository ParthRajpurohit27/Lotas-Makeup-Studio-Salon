General Queries answered

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this salon strictly for females?

Yes. Lotas Makeup Studio & Salon is an exclusive sanctuary designed strictly for ladies. Male entry is not allowed inside the lounge or waiting areas. This policy ensures total privacy, security, and absolute comfort for our female clients.

Do I need an appointment, or can I walk in?

Walk-ins are always welcome! However, to avoid waiting times (especially during weekends and wedding seasons), we highly recommend pre-scheduling your visit online through our Booking page or calling us directly.

What products and cosmetic brands do you use?

We prioritize skin health and hair texture. That's why we use only high-quality professional international brands, including Matrix Professional hair cosmetics, L'Oreal, and premium O3+ skin facial sets.

Is the parlour air-conditioned?

Yes, all our treatment rooms, styling chairs, and manicure desks are situated in fully air-conditioned spaces. You will enjoy a completely comfortable, cool, and relaxing spa visit.

Where are you located exactly?

We are located on the First Floor of Sarvdhram B Sector, Kolar Road, Bhopal. You can find us right near the Vishal Mega Mart. Directions and live calls are available on our maps mockup above.
